Global agriculture is a multi-trillion-dollar ecosystem under pressure from
resource scarcity, climate change, a growing population and changing consumer
expectations — and it remains, in the [AgStack Foundation's](https://agstack.org)
own words, "massively inefficient." Much of that inefficiency is not agronomic but
digital: the same field boundaries, crop models and supply-chain records get
rebuilt from scratch by every organisation that needs them, each behind its own
interface, none of them interoperable.

AgStack exists to remove that duplicated effort. It is a [Linux
Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/) project building **free, re-usable,
open and specialised digital infrastructure for agricultural data and
applications** — shared components that anyone, from a smallholder cooperative to
a multinational agribusiness, can build on rather than reinvent. The aim is a
common, community-maintained substrate for digital agriculture: public
infrastructure that lowers the cost of entry, makes systems interoperable by
default, and leaves the differentiating work to the people using it.

These pages document the projects that make up that infrastructure. Each section
belongs to a separate repository and is maintained by the people who work on it.
